Rushia's birthday!!

I didn't really get to know Rushia. My proper introduction to VTubers was around
November 2021 with holoMyth. But I truly consider Febuary 24th, 2022 as the day
I was in it for real. Rushia's termination [termination](https://web.archive.org/web/20220224061228/https://cover-corp.com/news/detail/20220224b/)
and finding Mikeneko's channel (and the subsequent [3TB](https://files.helpimnotdrowning.net/__UNFINISHED%20Uruha%20Rushia/)
that Rushia's channel occupies on my server) changed everything.

This drawing dresses Rushia in the [#きゅるるん大作戦](https://operation-heartful-cuties.hololivepro.com/)
outfit (the 3rd gen live performance that coincidentally led into Mikeneko's 3D
live). It originally had a purely white background with a soft, comforting glow
on her back. But, while browsing the art and birthday tags ([#潤羽るしあ生誕祭](https://x.com/hashtag/%E6%BD%A4%E7%BE%BD%E3%82%8B%E3%81%97%E3%81%82%E7%94%9F%E8%AA%95%E7%A5%AD)
and [#絵クロマンサー](https://x.com/hashtag/%E7%B5%B5%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%9E%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B5%E3%83%BC)
) , I saw an old post, featuring a butterfly on a sunset(?) sky. I threw on a
similar sky gradient and it blew it away. The butterfly with trail was also
last-minute, but I like how it lays as well. The touch on the eyes (which came
from the start!) is one of my favourite details.

The more full-size body and outfit was definitely more taxing to draw, but with
the way the long shadow hides the light, I think it was worth it. I'll try to
do more of this!
